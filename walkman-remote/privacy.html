Walkman Remote: Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Walkman Remote doesn't collect, store or share your personal data. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. All it does is talk to your Walkman over Bluetooth.

Who's behind it

Walkman Remote is an Android app made by Lulalab (lulalab.pt), a one-person project. "I" on this page means me, the developer. For anything about this policy, email lula.sousa@gmail.com.

Bluetooth

When you tap Connect, your phone starts advertising itself as a Bluetooth remote so your Walkman can find it. While the remote is on:

  • Nearby Bluetooth devices can see that advertisement, which includes your phone's Bluetooth name.
  • Once your Walkman connects, the app sends it your button presses and your phone's battery level, which the Walkman can show as the remote's battery.
  • The link between the phone and the Walkman isn't encrypted, so a nearby device listening in could see those same button presses and battery level. Nothing else about you or your phone is sent.

Tap Disconnect, or stop the remote from its notification, and the advertising stops.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to advertise as the remote and accept the Walkman's connection.
  • Notifications: for the media controls and the notification Android requires while the remote runs in the background.

The app doesn't ask for internet access, location, contacts, files, the camera or the microphone.

On your phone

The app saves no files and no settings. For troubleshooting, it writes connection events to Android's system log, including the Bluetooth addresses of devices that connect. That log stays on your phone. The app never sends it anywhere.

Buying the app

The app is sold through Google Play, and Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy. I never see your card details. Google sends me sales reports with order details, such as the order number, date, price and country. I use them only for accounting, taxes and refunds, and keep them as long as tax law requires.

The copy from Google Play includes Google's check that it was installed from Play. That check runs between the Google Play app on your phone and Google. It tells me nothing about you.

Emailing me

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply, and I don't keep them longer than I need to help you.

Sharing

I don't sell or share personal data. The app contains no third-party code for ads, analytics or crash reporting.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights

The app collects nothing, so there is no app data to access or delete. For order records or emails, you can ask me for a copy, a correction or deletion at lula.sousa@gmail.com. Records that tax law makes me keep can't be deleted early. In the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority. In Portugal, that's the CNPD.

Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If a future version ever needs your data, this policy will say so before that version is released - it won't.
